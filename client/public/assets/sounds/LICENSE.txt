Sounds kept for the table.

purpose. Libraries to audition AGAINST are not checked in — they are unpacked
into the sample-libraries directory beside the game state, outside the repo.

Anything added here needs its source and its licence written down beside it.
A file whose licence nobody recorded is a file nobody can ship.
