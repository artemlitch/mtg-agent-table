Sounds kept for the table.

Everything in here survived an audition in the sound lab and is checked in on
purpose. Libraries to audition AGAINST are not checked in — they are unpacked
into the sample-libraries directory beside the game state, outside the repo.

	sword-unsheathe*.wav    RPG Sound Pack, by artisticdude
	                        https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
	                        CC0 — public domain, no attribution required

	bookFlip3.ogg           RPG Audio, by Kenney Vleugels (kenney.nl)
	                        https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
	                        CC0 — public domain, no attribution required

	blor-1..6.wav           Recorded by the owner of this repo on a phone and
	                        cut into six hits. Nobody else's to license.

	hit_1..5.wav            Cut from "Long Two-Handed Axe Flesh Hit"
	block_01..05.wav        Cut from "Metal Shield Block Hits"
	                        both: Justsoundeffects, Melee Weapons, in the
	                        Sonniss GDC 2024 Game Audio Bundle
	                        https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc
	                        https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/

	                        NOT public domain. Royalty-free and commercially
	                        usable, no attribution required, unlimited
	                        projects — but the licence turns on the difference
	                        between a project and a sound library. It permits
	                        selling them "as incorporated into licensee
	                        project" and forbids selling "any of the sound
	                        effects as they come".
	                        Inside this game they are incorporated. A public
	                        repository of loose .wav files is closer to
	                        handing them out as they come, so if this
	                        checkout is ever published, these two sets are the
	                        files to think about first — everything else here
	                        is CC0 or the owner's own.
	                        The licence also forbids using them to train
	                        machine-learning models.

Anything added here needs its source and its licence written down beside it.
A file whose licence nobody recorded is a file nobody can ship.
